E-Commerce WhatsApp FAQs

Yes. Through Meta's official Cloud API, you can send pre-approved marketing templates to opted-in shoppers. InstantFlow scales your sending tiers from 1,000 to 10,000, 100,000, and unlimited daily messages while maintaining your number's Green Quality rating.
