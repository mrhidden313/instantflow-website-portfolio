Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if my business WhatsApp is banned?

If your business WhatsApp is banned, do not panic. First, open the WhatsApp Business app and tap "Request a Review". State that you are a legitimate business and were unaware of any policy violations. To prevent this from ever happening again, you must switch from unofficial apps to an Official Meta API Provider like InstantFlow immediately.

How long does a WhatsApp Business ban last?

A temporary ban (usually caused by sending too many messages too fast) lasts anywhere from 8 to 24 hours. However, if you are caught using unofficial software like GB WhatsApp or unauthorized Chrome extensions, the ban is often permanent. Using InstantFlow's Official Cloud API ensures your number is never temporarily or permanently banned for bulk messaging.

How can I fix my banned WhatsApp number?

You can try to fix it by contacting WhatsApp support via email (smb_web@support.whatsapp.com) or requesting a review inside the app. However, recovery is rare for unofficial API usage. The only permanent fix for bulk messaging is registering a fresh number with the InstantFlow Platform which connects natively to Meta's secure cloud infrastructure.

What is the 24 hour rule for WhatsApp Business?

The 24-hour rule is a Meta policy stating that a business can reply freely to a user's message within a 24-hour window from the user's last message. After 24 hours, the business must use pre-approved Template Messages to re-engage the customer. InstantFlow handles this automatically, preventing your account from violating this strict policy.

No! You can safely send 100,000+ messages without a Green Tick verification. While a Green Tick looks professional, our underlying Anti-Ban technology protects your number entirely independent of your verification status.
