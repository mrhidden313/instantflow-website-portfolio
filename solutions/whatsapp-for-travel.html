Hospitality Benchmark

Why WhatsApp is the Standard for Modern Travelers

International travelers frequently operate on mobile roaming data where downloading heavy dedicated apps is inconvenient. WhatsApp is pre-installed on over 2 billion devices worldwide. Utilizing InstantFlow's official Cloud API enables tour operators and hotel chains to communicate effortlessly without requiring guest app downloads or SMS roaming charges.

Global Roaming Delivery & Cross-Border Transit SLA

International travelers frequently transit across telecom boundaries, switch SIM cards, or connect to foreign Wi-Fi networks where legacy SMS notifications consistently fail. InstantFlow leverages WhatsApp's global data-layer infrastructure to guarantee 99.8% message delivery across 180+ countries regardless of mobile carrier roaming status. Automated fallback webhooks monitor delivery receipts in real time, retrying unconfirmed dispatches across secondary routing channels when flight gates or schedule revisions occur.
