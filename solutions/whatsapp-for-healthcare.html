Knowledge Base

Healthcare WhatsApp Automation FAQs

Is sending medical reports over WhatsApp compliant with patient privacy standards?

Yes, when implemented through official Meta Cloud API infrastructure. All messages are protected with end-to-end encryption. Sensitive diagnostic PDFs can be further secured with date-of-birth password protection.

Can multiple clinic branches or departments share one WhatsApp Business API account?

Yes. InstantFlow provides multi-branch routing. Patients can choose their branch (e.g. London, Dubai, Islamabad) from an interactive menu, and chats are automatically routed to the corresponding department staff.

How does InstantFlow handle medical emergencies after clinic hours?

You can configure intelligent keyword triggers (e.g., 'Emergency', 'Urgent', 'Pain'). The system immediately replies with emergency helpline numbers, nearest hospital navigation pins, and sends instant SMS/push alerts to on-call medical officers.
