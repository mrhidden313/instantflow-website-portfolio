Knowledge Base

Real Estate WhatsApp CRM FAQs

Can my real estate brokers use their personal phones while keeping customer data in company CRM?

Yes. Through the InstantFlow Android App and multi-agent dashboard, brokers chat from the official corporate WhatsApp Business API number. Client phone numbers and chat transcripts remain permanently secured inside your company CRM even if an agent leaves.

How does InstantFlow handle international buyers in the UK, Europe, and Gulf countries?

InstantFlow supports international number formats natively. You can configure multi-lingual auto-replies in English, Arabic, and Urdu, and share high-res video walkthroughs and currency-converted payment plans automatically based on the prospect's country code.

Will sending bulk property launch notifications get our agency's WhatsApp banned?

No. Unofficial tools get banned because they lack Meta Cloud API compliance. InstantFlow routes all off-plan broadcasts through official Meta approved templates with smart micro-delay throttling, safeguarding your agency's phone number reputation.
