Knowledge Base

Education WhatsApp Automation FAQs

Can admissions counselors chat with prospective students from their personal phones?

Yes, using the InstantFlow Android App. Counselors send and receive WhatsApp messages using the university's official verified number, keeping their personal cell number completely private while archiving all communication in the institution's CRM.

Can we send bulk broadcast updates to 20,000+ enrolled students without phone number bans?

Yes. Through Meta's official WhatsApp Business API, InstantFlow scales broadcast volume tiers from 1,000 to unlimited daily messages with built-in algorithmic delay throttling, ensuring zero interruption during critical campus alerts.

Does InstantFlow integrate with fee collection gateways in India, Pakistan, and the UK?

Yes. InstantFlow supports dynamic payment links with Razorpay, Cashfree, Stripe, JazzCash, and 1Link, allowing parents to complete tuition fee payments directly from their phone with instant receipt dispatch.
